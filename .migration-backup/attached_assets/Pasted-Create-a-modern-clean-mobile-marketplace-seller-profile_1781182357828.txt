Create a modern, clean mobile marketplace seller profile page inspired by the Google Discover feed aesthetic. The design should be minimal, content-first, card-based, and highly visual with smooth spacing and soft shadows.

🧩 Overall Style

* Google Discover-inspired UI (clean, airy, content-focused)
* White or light background with subtle gray dividers
* Soft rounded cards (12–16px radius)
* Smooth scrolling feed layout
* High emphasis on product images
* Modern 2026 mobile UI aesthetic
* Typography similar to Google Sans (clean, readable, bold headings)

⸻

👤 Header Section (Seller Identity)

At the top, design a compact but strong seller identity block:

* Circular seller avatar (left aligned)
* Seller name (bold, large text)
* “Verified Seller” badge (blue check style)
* Location under name (light gray text)
* Message / Contact icon button
* Small stats row:
    * ⭐ Rating (e.g. 4.8)
    * 📦 Total sales
    * ⏱ Response time

⸻

📊 Quick Action Tabs (Sticky Scroll)

Below header, add a sticky horizontal tab bar:

* All Listings
* Featured
* New Arrivals
* Reviews
* About

Tabs should behave like Google Discover category chips (scrollable horizontally, active state highlighted).

⸻

🛍️ Main Content Feed (Discover Style Product Cards)

Display seller products in a Google Discover-style vertical feed of large cards:

Each product card should include:

* Large product image (dominant visual)
* Product title (bold, 1–2 lines max)
* Price (highlighted)
* Small seller tag (optional redundant but subtle)
* Location or delivery info (light text)
* bookmark icon
* “View details” CTA button (minimal)

Card Style:

* Rounded corners
* Soft shadow or elevation
* White card on light gray background
* Spaced like Google Discover articles

⸻

⭐ Featured Section (Optional Highlight)

Add a “Featured by Seller” section:

* Horizontal scroll carousel
* Larger cards than normal feed
* “Best Seller” or “Hot Deal” badges

⸻

📝 About Section (Minimal)

Clean text block:

* Short seller bio (2–3 lines max)
* Policies (delivery, return)
* Contact info

⸻

💬 Reviews Section

* Vertical list of review cards
* Star rating + short comment
* User avatar + date
* Clean spacing (no clutter)

⸻

📱 Interaction Design

* Smooth infinite scroll feed
* Sticky header collapses slightly on scroll
* Tabs remain visible while scrolling
* Tap animations are subtle (scale 0.98)
* Lazy loading images for performance

⸻

🎨 UI Feel Keywords

“Google Discover meets Pinterest marketplace meets modern social commerce app”
